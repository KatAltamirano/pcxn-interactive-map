Welcome to the Portland Creative Xchange Network!

We are so glad you are here! This map is a living, growing portrait of Portland's creative economy. Our data is a work in progress and intentionally imperfect. You will find many listings with incomplete or unverified information. We invite our community to help us build something more accurate and representative of the creative fabric of our city.

If you find your enterprise on the map, we'd love your help completing or correcting your listing. If you don't see yourself, please add your business so we can include you.

About Our Data Structure

We're using the data taxonomy developed by the New England Foundation for the Arts through their CreativeGround project. Their definitions for business types and activities and services are the standards we reference for organizing our directory.
